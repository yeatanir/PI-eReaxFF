Shape (pair, gap 3.5-5.0): a **narrow pit about 0.2 A wide exactly on each apex Ag** (the paired electron), steep walls, an **intrinsic barrier of 9-12 kcal/mol only ~0.3-0.4 A from the pit centre** on the gap side, and a broad soft region between the units (a shallow well or plateau, degenerate with the pits at gap 3.5-4.0, 2.3 kcal/mol above at 4.5). Consequences: (i) the two sites ARE degenerate for the symmetric pair (cluster + single Ag+ is rejected: acceptor 24 kcal/mol up at 4 A, absent at 5 A); (ii) the intrinsic barrier (~10 kcal/mol, ~17 kT) is already 3x larger than the prompt's 3 kcal/mol artificial barrier, and it sits near the donor/acceptor sites, not at the midpoint; the prompt's midpoint Gaussian barrier acts on the soft mid-region and the pit-to-mid transitions are governed by the intrinsic ~10 kcal/mol step. The demonstration should therefore either treat the soft middle region as the donor/acceptor halves (barrier at its centre), or size/position the barrier knowing this. Recommended geometry for Part B: **symmetric pair, gap 4.0-4.5 A**.
