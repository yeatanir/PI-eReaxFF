## A1 - several beads through LAMMPS + Metal-Reax: PASS (run)
P = 8, 8 clients (one per bead), NVT PILE-L, 300 steps; every client received requests. Each client dumped x, f (`%.16g`) and PE at every LAMMPS step.
Observation: `fix ipi` runs **3 LAMMPS force evaluations per i-PI request**; two of them repeat the same positions (identical x and F). Harmless for correctness, a 3x cost overhead.
10 distinct bead geometries (spread over the 8 clients, random times), compared three ways (`A1_compare_output.txt`):
| Comparison | max abs force difference | relative to max\|F\| (~75 kcal/mol/A) |
|---|---|---|
| client force vs the same plugin evaluated offline at the identical (unrounded) positions | 1.4e-11 kcal/mol/A | 2e-13 |
| plugin offline vs exact rebuilt ereac, both at positions rounded to 5 decimals (the legacy geo format f10.5) | 3.3e-9 kcal/mol/A (energy 7.8e-12 relative) | 4.9e-11 |
| client force at the unrounded positions vs ereac at rounded positions | up to 8.6e-3 kcal/mol/A | 1.4e-4 |
The last row is not an error of the plugin: it is the 1e-5 A position rounding of the legacy file format times the stiff electron well (k ~ 1150 kcal/mol/A^2 gives ~6e-3 kcal/mol/A for 5e-6 A). The criterion "1e-10 relative if positions are rounded identically" is met (4.9e-11).

## A2 - mass handling: PASS (run + read)
- **Source (read):** the engine derives species flags only from the **ffield** mass (`src/io/ffield_parser.cpp:237`, `derive_species_flags(s.name, s.mass)`); the adapter (`plugin/adapter/*`) never reads `atom->mass`/`rmass`. So the LAMMPS data-file mass cannot influence the engine.
- **Test 1 (run):** same 3 geometries, LAMMPS data-file El mass 1.0 / 0.5 / 0.1 / 25.0 amu (25 is above the legacy `mass > 21` threshold): energy and forces **bitwise identical** (max difference 0.0).
- **Test 2 (run):** i-PI El mass 1.0 / 0.5 / 0.1 amu (P = 2 runs): the restart file shows El masses 1822.89 / 911.44 / 182.29 a.u. (1.0 / 0.5 / 0.1 amu) and Ag 107.8682 amu; step-0 positions and client forces are identical across the three masses.
(`A2_output.txt`.) The ffield mass column was never edited.

## A3 - conserved quantity from an equilibrated start
**Definition (read):** i-PI prints `conserved = econs / nbeads` (`ipi/engine/properties.py:1573`; header: "per bead"), where `econs = spring + kinetic + sum over beads of V (+ thermostat/constraint terms)`. All spans below are of the printed per-bead quantity, in kcal/mol; "total" = x P.

**Setup (run):** Ag7_1 (7 Ag classical, 7 El rings, 1 amu), 300 K, dt 0.25 fs (omega*dt = 0.69 fs^-1 x 0.25 = 0.17 < 0.2), PILE-L (centroid tau 100 fs) for 15 ps (60 000 steps) -> NVE 2 ps from the equilibrated i-PI checkpoint (mode edited to NVE), at dt = 0.25 and 0.125 fs from the same state (plus dt 0.0625 as a diagnostic). One LAMMPS client + the i-PI server per run (CPU cap). One seed and one equilibrated state per P. P = 1 was equilibrated for 3 ps (no ring). Pipeline: `a3_pipeline.py`; raw numbers `A3_results.json`.

**Equilibration check (El radius of gyration, mean over 7 electrons, six 2.5 ps blocks):**
| P | blocks (A) | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | 0.140, 0.145, 0.146, 0.147, 0.1505, 0.1502 | last two blocks agree to 0.2 %; first-to-last +7 % (slow growth that flattens) |
| 32 | 0.161, 0.165, 0.165, 0.165, 0.165, 0.168 | flat within 1-2 %, last block 1.5 % higher |
(A first attempt with only 3 ps for P = 8 was NOT stationary: 0.132 -> 0.151 A over 3 ps; it was discarded and redone with 15 ps. The measured ring size, 0.14-0.17 A, is larger than the harmonic-well estimate 0.088 A: the El well is soft/anharmonic away from the minimum, k falls from 50 to 27 eV/A^2 at 0.2 A.)

**Verdict against the criteria:**
- P = 8: span/bead is 1.95 x the P = 1 span at dt 0.25 (< 3): PASS. At dt 0.125: 1.1 x.
- P = 32: **3.66 x** the P = 1 span at dt 0.25 (above the threshold of ~3). Explained (not hidden): the span has two parts. (i) A fast step-to-step ripple of the extended Hamiltonian that falls steeply with the timestep (P = 32: 3.5e-2 -> 4.6e-3 -> 9.7e-4 for dt 0.25 -> 0.125 -> 0.0625, i.e. 7.5x then 4.7x), which dominates at dt 0.25 where the highest ring mode has omega_max*dt = 2 P kT dt/hbar = 0.63; (ii) a smooth, **timestep-independent** slow wander (correlation time 36-65 fs, no discrete jumps > 6 sigma) of 0.10 kcal/mol at P = 32 versus 0.045 at P = 1 and 0.03 at P = 8. At dt = 0.125 the P = 32 span is 2.4 x and at dt = 0.0625 it is 2.1 x the P = 1 span (both < 3). So **P = 32 passes at dt <= 0.125 fs, not at 0.25 fs.**
- Halving dt: span falls at every P (x0.78 at P = 1, which sits on the slow floor; x0.44 at P = 8; x0.51 at P = 32): PASS.
- **Not explained:** the origin of the dt-independent slow wander (about 2.2 x larger at P = 32 than at P = 1). It is smooth, not made of force-field steps. Candidate causes, none tested: legacy force not being exactly the gradient of its energy where the rings sample the soft tail (the legacy single-precision literals are known), unit-constant differences between fix ipi and i-PI, or the El cutoff/taper treatment at larger bead spread. It does not shrink with dt, so reducing dt cannot remove it.
- Conserved quantity: **per bead** (confirmed in source), see above; spans in this table are in those units.

## Practical numbers
- Cost with 1 client (LAMMPS) + server, Ag7_1: P = 8 equilibration 15 ps = 327 s; P = 32: 2 ps NVE at dt 0.25 = 58 s, at dt 0.125 = 117 s, 15 ps equilibration ~ 25 min. About 5 ms per bead-evaluation, of which `fix ipi` triples the LAMMPS work (3 force evaluations per request).
- For the tunneling runs use dt <= 0.125 fs at P >= 32 (at 1 amu); at lighter masses omega and P grow (A6).
